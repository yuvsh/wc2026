# Floodlit

## A Design Philosophy for WC2026

---

### I. The Atmosphere

The stadium at 21:00. Ninety thousand people in darkness, the pitch a luminous rectangle of impossible green cut by white chalk lines. This is the emotional substrate — not the game itself, but the *electricity before it*. Floodlit is born from that liminal moment: the space between anticipation and result, between prediction and reality. Every surface in this system carries that charge. Dark grounds lit from within. Geometry that holds energy like a coil.

### II. Space and Form

The grid is sacred. Like the penalty box, the centre circle, the arc — every line on the pitch is precise and load-bearing. Compositions breathe in the negative space between elements, treating emptiness as tactical territory, not vacancy. Cards and surfaces float against deep grounds rather than resting on flat planes. Form is minimal but never cold: each shape has the weight of something that matters. The craft must be visible in the exactitude of every margin, every radius, every alignment — the work of someone who measured twice and measured again.

### III. Color and Light

A nocturnal palette: near-black navy (`#0F172A`) as the primary ground, alive with depth. Against it, teal (`#0D9488`) functions as the floodlight beam — the single source of illumination that makes everything else readable. Gold (`#EF9F27`) is the trophy: rare, earned, never decorative. It appears only where victory is named — the first-place row, the bingo moment, the podium peak. Secondary surfaces use a controlled cool gray scale, never warm, always suggesting concrete and steel and stadium infrastructure. The palette is the work of painstaking calibration: each value chosen not for beauty alone but for the precise emotional register it occupies in the hierarchy.

### IV. Scale and Rhythm

Scale creates meaning before content does. The match score — two digits separated by a dash — is the most important number in the system and must be treated as a monument. Rank numerals carry weight proportional to their significance: 1 is larger than 2, which is larger than 3. The countdown timer is small when time is plentiful; it grows and pulses as the window closes. This rhythmic scaling — quiet to urgent, resting to alive — is the visual heartbeat of the system. It must feel like it was composed by a master who understood that rhythm, not decoration, is what makes a design feel alive.

### V. Craft and Typography

The Hebrew letterforms of Heebo are the voice of this system — not an afterthought but the visual signature of a specific community gathering around a specific event. Letters are set with the care of a letterpress compositor: tracking tightened on bold display moments, leading generous on readable body, weights chosen for optical not mathematical equivalence. Numbers are always tabular. Hierarchy is expressed through three variables — weight, size, opacity — never through color alone. Every typographic decision is the product of deep expertise and deliberate restraint. Nothing decorates. Everything communicates.
